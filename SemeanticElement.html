Italian dishes

Spaghetti

1. Take a large pot and add water to it. Boil the water and add salt and a drop of oil.
2. Place the spaghetti in the water and keep it in water till it become little soft.
3. Drain the water and keep spaghetti aside.
4. Chop your favorite vegetables into thin slices or small pieces (common choices include onions, garlic, capsicum/bell peppers, carrots, and corn).
5. Heat olive oil or cooking oil in a large pan or skillet over medium heat.
6. Add minced garlic and chopped onions, sautéing for 1 to 2 minutes until fragrant and translucent.
7. Add the rest of your chopped vegetables and cook for 5 to 7 minutes until they are tender yet retain a slight crunch.
8. Let veggies be cooked and then add Spaghetti and seasonings.

Chinese recipes

Noodles

  • 1. Boil the noodles in water until they are soft.
  • 2. Drain the noodles and keep them aside.
  • 3. Heat oil in a pan.
  • 4. Add onion, carrot and capsicum.
  • 5. Fry the vegetables for a few minutes.
  • 6. Add soy sauce and chilli sauce.
  • 7. Add the boiled noodles.
  • 8. Mix everything well.
  • 9. Add salt and black pepper.
  • 10. Cook for 2-3 minutes and serve hot.
